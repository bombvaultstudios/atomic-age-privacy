Privacy Policy for Atomic Age Icon Pack

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app "Atomic Age Icon Pack" (package name: com.joshuawhite.atomicageiconpack), published by Joshua White ("I", "me"). It explains what information the app handles and how.

Summary

Atomic Age Icon Pack is an icon pack for Android launchers. It does not require an account, and I do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

Information I collect

I do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up or login, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

How the app works

The app supplies icon images to your launcher. To show which of your installed apps have matching icons, the app may check the list of apps installed on your device. This check happens only on your device. The list is not sent to me or to anyone else.

Third-party services

The app may use the following third-party services, which can process limited technical data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play services: used to distribute the app and, where enabled, to verify that it was installed from Google Play. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • OneSignal: used to deliver optional push notifications (for example, to tell you about new icons). OneSignal may process a device identifier and basic device information to do this. See OneSignal's Privacy Policy. You can turn off notifications for the app in your device settings.

Data sharing

I do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties, other than the service providers described above.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
